02 Executive summary

We work inside the revenue cycle you already run.

01 / Context

The pressure

Hospitals face reimbursement challenges, rising operational costs, complex payer requirements, and growing uncompensated care obligations.

02 / Opportunity

The gap

In ED, trauma and other encounters, Workers' Compensation, Third-Party Liability, auto and other responsible-party coverage can go unidentified or unpursued by traditional workflows.

03 / Support

What LaborForce adds

Specialized payer identification, patient engagement, revenue auditing, payer verification, and lien administration support.

04 / Outcome

The goal

Identify additional reimbursement, improve revenue realization, and reduce avoidable administrative work — without replacing current systems or teams.

How it works
  1. 01Technology screening

    Injury-related encounters are flagged from existing registration and billing data.

  2. 02Specialist review

    Analysts determine the likely responsible payer and what information is missing.

  3. 03Direct patient contact

    Dedicated staff confirm the facts and close gaps, respecting each patient's choices.

  4. 04Follow-through

    Billing corrections, lien support, and payer follow-up until the account resolves.

03 The challenge

Revenue that traditional workflows may overlook.

Patients often arrive after workplace incidents, motor vehicle accidents, or injuries involving another liable party. Registration and billing workflows may not consistently answer:

  1. 01

    Was the injury work-related?

  2. 02

    Is a motor vehicle or other third party responsible?

  3. 03

    Should another insurer or payer be investigated?

  4. 04

    Does the hospital have the right claim, attorney, and payer information?

  5. 05

    Is a hospital lien available, documented, and followed up?

  6. 06

    Are unresolved cases investigated after discharge?

Where the revenue goes
  1. Injury-related encounterWorkplace injury, motor vehicle accident, or incident involving a third party
  2. Responsible payer missedNot identified at registration, or identified but not followed through
  3. Absorbed by the hospitalWritten off as bad debt or uncompensated care
The consequence

Valid reimbursement opportunities may remain unidentified, incorrectly routed, delayed, or insufficiently pursued.

04 The LaborForce solution

A specialized operational layer.
Five critical functions.

  1. 01
    Proactive Revenue Identification

    Review injury-related encounters, identify potentially responsible payers, and prioritize accounts for investigation.

  2. 02
    Dedicated Patient Engagement

    Outreach to verify injury circumstances and collect missing claim, employer, insurer, or attorney information.

  3. 03
    Payer Verification & RCM Coordination

    Coordinate findings with registration, billing, and revenue cycle teams for payer determination and follow-up.

  4. 04
    Revenue Auditing & Reconciliation

    Monitor unresolved cases, flag missing or inconsistent information, and track case status and reimbursement.

  5. 05
    Lien Administration & Recovery

    Support approved lien documentation, follow-up, and reimbursement tracking within the legal and contractual framework.

LaborForce works alongside your billing and collections teams.

08 Proposed partnership

90-day pilot.
John H. Stroger, Jr. Hospital.

  1. Phase 01Days 1 – 15
    15 of 90 days

    Discovery & Baseline

    Review current injury-related registration, payer identification, billing, and follow-up. Agree on pilot scope, eligibility criteria, compliance requirements, and baseline measures.

  2. Phase 02Days 16 – 60
    45 of 90 days

    Identification & Investigation

    Evaluate designated encounters, conduct authorized patient outreach, identify alternative payer sources, and coordinate verified findings with existing hospital teams.

  3. Phase 03Days 61 – 90
    30 of 90 days

    Validation & Review

    Reconcile outcomes, review billing corrections and collections, assess operational impact, and recommend continued monitoring and potential expansion.

How to read the pilot

Many claims take longer than 90 days to resolve. The pilot measures leading indicators and any collections realized; final financial performance is reviewed as the identified account cohort matures.

Cook County Health provides
  • Executive sponsor and agreed pilot scope
  • ED volume, payer mix, and current TPL/WC workflow data
  • Compliance requirements and data-access terms
LaborForce provides
  • Screening, specialist review, and case investigation
  • Dedicated patient outreach and lien administration support
  • Joint reporting at days 30, 60, and 90
